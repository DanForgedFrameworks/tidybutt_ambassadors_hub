Possible roles

Different ways to support as an ambassador

Not everyone has to do everything. People can contribute in ways that suit their confidence, interests, availability, and strengths.

Ambassadors may be involved in different types of activities depending on their interests, confidence, and availability. These roles may overlap, and people may do more than one.

This page gives you a clearer look at the kinds of ambassador roles that may exist within Tidy Butt.

Possible ambassador roles

Explore the different ways ambassadors may support Tidy Butt and its community.

Community Activity Ambassador

Supporting community activities and sessions, welcoming new people, helping people feel comfortable in groups, and encouraging participation.

  • Supporting community activities and sessions
  • Welcoming new people
  • Helping people feel comfortable in groups
  • Encouraging participation
  • Helping with basic organisation on the day

Outreach or Awareness Ambassador

Helping people understand what Tidy Butt offers and sharing information about available support and opportunities.

  • Speaking to community groups
  • Sharing information about Tidy Butt
  • Attending events or community days
  • Helping people understand what support is available

Events and Support Ambassador

Supporting events, helping them run smoothly, and helping participants feel safe, welcome, and included.

  • Helping run events
  • Supporting fundraising activities
  • Helping with logistics and organisation
  • Supporting participants during events

Admin or Coordination Support

Helping behind the scenes with communication, coordination, planning, and practical organisation.

  • Helping with communication
  • Supporting coordination of activities
  • Helping organise sessions or resources
  • Supporting behind-the-scenes work
