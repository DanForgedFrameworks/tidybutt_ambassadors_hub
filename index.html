Welcome to the Tidy Butt Ambassadors Hub

A welcoming starting point for people wanting to support connection, conversation, and community wellbeing.

This hub is being developed to support Tidy Butt ambassadors, and anybody who wants to find out more about becoming involved in supporting Tidy Butt.

It brings together clear information, practical guidance, useful resources, and supportive signposting to help people understand the ambassador role and how they may be able to contribute.

So you want to be an ambassador?

Start here to explore what being a Tidy Butt ambassador could involve, what to expect, possible ways to get involved, and important considerations when supporting people and communities around mental health and wellbeing.
