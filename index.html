Welcome to the Tidy Butt Ambassadors Hub

A welcoming starting point for people who want to support connection, conversation, and community wellbeing.

This hub is being developed to support Tidy Butt ambassadors with clear, accessible guidance about the role, what to expect, and how different ambassador pathways may work in practice.

Use this hub to explore the purpose of the ambassador community, possible ways to get involved, first steps into the role, and the support available as the programme grows.

This space is intended to feel clear, supportive, and easy to navigate. It will bring together key information, practical guidance, useful documents, and future resources in one place.
