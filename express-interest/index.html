Express your interest

A simple first step if you would like to get involved.

If you are interested in supporting Tidy Butt as an ambassador or volunteer, you can use the prompts below to help shape your first message.

There is no pressure to write loads. A short message is enough.

What to include

  • Introduce yourself
  • Why would you like to get involved?
  • Tell us a bit about your interests, skills, or any ideas you have
