Support and contact

Supporting others is important, but so is knowing where support comes from and who to contact when something feels bigger than your role.

Tidy Butt’s contact page is there for people who want to volunteer, book a talk, or ask a general question. If the need is specifically about counselling, Tidy Butt asks people to use the dedicated counselling route instead.

Course support

For support related to the ambassador hub itself, your first point of contact is this hub, the information on the Tidy Butt website, and the wider Tidy Butt tribe around you.

  • Use the hub pages and downloads to check expectations, boundaries, and next steps.
  • Use the Tidy Butt contact page for general questions, volunteering queries, or getting in touch directly.
  • Use the Tidy Butt resources page if someone needs wider support, signposting, or urgent mental health information.

If you are in crisis or need urgent help

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999.

Tidy Butt’s resources page also signposts people to trusted mental health and crisis support services. This includes Samaritans, Rethink Mental Illness, Papyrus HOPELINEUK, NHS Wales, Mind, CALL, and CALM.

Not every urgent situation is the same. Some people may need immediate emergency help. Others may need a listening service, mental health guidance, suicide prevention support, or a trusted helpline in Wales. That is why it is important to use the wider support resources rather than relying on a single instruction.

Trusted support

Useful support services signposted by Tidy Butt

The Tidy Butt resources page includes a range of trusted organisations for different kinds of support.

  • Samaritans for anyone who needs to talk.
  • Rethink Mental Illness for support and information for people affected by mental health problems.
  • Papyrus HOPELINEUK for confidential support when someone is at risk of suicide or when someone is worried about them.
  • NHS Wales for non-emergency medical helpline support in Wales.
  • Mind for information and signposting.
  • CALL for mental health information and support in Wales.
  • CALM for listening support by telephone or online.

These services are listed on Tidy Butt’s resources page, so the safest approach is to send people there for the latest contact details and support options.
