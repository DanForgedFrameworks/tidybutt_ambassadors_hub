So you want to be an ambassador

Shwmae, and thank you for being here.

Choosing to become an ambassador is not just volunteering your time. It is choosing to stand alongside people, to listen, to support, to encourage, and sometimes simply to be present when someone needs another human being nearby.

You are not expected to be a counsellor, therapist, social worker, or rescuer. You are a supportive person within a community who helps people feel welcome, included, and not alone.

Our mission

“We educate, empower, and support individuals to better understand their mental health and wellbeing. Through open, honest conversations and a strong sense of community, we help people build confidence, find connection, and develop the tools to face life’s challenges. We believe good mental health is for everyone.”
Does this mission resonate with you? Have you experienced these challenges directly or indirectly? Do you believe connection and conversation can make a difference?

Why this matters: Being an ambassador often starts with understanding why this matters to you, and why you want to stand alongside other people in a supportive, community-focused way.

Tidy Butt is a community-focused organisation that supports people through connection, activity, conversation, and community engagement.

The focus is on helping people take positive steps, however small, towards improved wellbeing, confidence, and social connection.

  • Loneliness or isolation
  • Anxiety or low mood
  • Loss of confidence
  • Life changes or difficult circumstances
  • Mental health challenges
  • Financial or socio-economic pressures
  • Periods of crisis

Tidy Butt is not a clinical service. It is a community, a support network, and a place where people can find connection, structure, encouragement, and opportunities to move forward.

Being a Tidy Butt volunteer or ambassador is not just about what you do, it is about the impact you create.

At its core, being an ambassador means:

  • Being welcoming
  • Being kind
  • Being reliable
  • Being calm
  • Being respectful
  • Being inclusive
  • Being supportive without trying to fix people
  • Helping people feel safe and comfortable
  • Helping people access activities, support, and community

You are not there to solve everyone’s problems. You are there to help people feel that they are not facing things alone.

Some of the people you meet may be going through very difficult periods in their lives. You may hear difficult stories, see people in distress, or meet people in crisis.

You are not a rescuer. Your role is to listen, support, signpost, encourage, stay calm, and maintain boundaries.

You may hear about bereavement, abuse, addiction, suicide attempts, self-harm, homelessness, financial crisis, or relationship breakdown. This can be emotionally difficult.

  • Do not carry this alone
  • Speak to the Tidy Butt team if something is worrying you
  • Understand your limits
  • Take breaks when needed
  • Look after your own wellbeing

If someone is in crisis, ambassadors are not expected to manage this alone. Stay calm, stay with the person if safe to do so, and contact the appropriate support.

  • You are allowed to say no
  • You are allowed to take breaks
  • You are not available 24/7
  • You should not give out personal contact details unless agreed
  • You should not promise things you cannot deliver
  • You should not take responsibility for someone else’s safety alone
  • You should ask for help when you need it

A burnt-out ambassador cannot help anyone. A supported ambassador can help many people.

Tidy Butt is not about hierarchy and titles. It is about people working together, supporting each other, and building something that helps others.

  • Part of a team
  • Supported
  • Trusted
  • Comfortable asking questions
  • Comfortable saying when something is difficult
  • Proud of what they are part of

This is a community and tribe effort. We support each other, learn from each other, and build something together that is bigger than any one person.

Possible ambassador roles

People can contribute in ways that suit their skills, confidence, and personal situation.

Community Activity Ambassador

Supporting community activities and sessions, welcoming new people, helping people feel comfortable in groups, and encouraging participation.

  • Welcoming new people
  • Helping people feel comfortable in groups
  • Encouraging participation
  • Helping with basic organisation on the day

Outreach or Awareness Ambassador

Helping people understand what Tidy Butt offers and sharing information about available support and opportunities.

  • Speaking to community groups
  • Sharing information about Tidy Butt
  • Attending events or community days
  • Helping people understand what support is available

Events and Support Ambassador

Supporting events, helping them run smoothly, and helping participants feel safe, welcome, and included.

  • Helping run events
  • Supporting fundraising activities
  • Helping with logistics and organisation
  • Supporting participants during events

Admin or Coordination Support

Helping behind the scenes with communication, coordination, planning, and practical organisation.

  • Helping with communication
  • Supporting coordination of activities
  • Helping organise sessions or resources
  • Supporting behind-the-scenes work

How ambassadors make a difference

Talking to someone who has not spoken to anyone all week
Helping someone attend their first group or activity
Listening to someone who is having a difficult time
Encouraging someone who lacks confidence
Helping someone feel part of a community
Being a familiar, friendly face
